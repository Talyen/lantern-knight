# Roadmap

Author new prototype scenes and build a cohesive progression loop. [Game design](game-design.md) owns design intent; this page tracks capability, priorities and acceptance.

Implemented means reachable in source, in progress means unfinished, planned means agreed direction, and needs design means a decision or playtest is required. These statuses do not certify current checks, visual polish or release availability. Supplied hero animation artwork remains TEST material pending visible playtesting.

Acceptance: a new player understands the controls, reads enemy actions, uses sword and lantern meaningfully, traverses connected authored areas, retries after death and saves/resumes reliably. Confirm through visible playtesting and applicable automated checks. Authored playable areas are part of this acceptance target; independent preview fixtures alone do not satisfy it. The opening does not need a chapter ending.

## Next

Design levels/stats, equipment/loot and permanent upgrades together, then implement a minimal reward-and-choice loop in authored prototype content. Resolve attributes, slots, reward cadence and relationships before implementation. Persist progression and retain it on death while retrying the encounter. See [character progression](game-design.md#character-progression).

## Later

Extend the adventure and authored rewards, equipment, upgrades and encounters after combat polish and the first progression loop. Destinations, bosses and speculative systems remain uncommitted; see [world structure](game-design.md#world-structure).

## Open design decisions

Lantern shape, cooldown, stagger, Burn cadence and recovery cancellation are chosen playtest defaults in the linked design sections. Weaknesses remain deferred; resolve their values and the progression relationships before extending those systems. Keep decisions in [Game design](game-design.md), then update the corresponding work here.
