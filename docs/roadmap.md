# Roadmap

Polish combat and presentation in the connected opening before extending the adventure. [Game design](game-design.md) owns design intent; this page tracks capability, priorities and acceptance.

Implemented means reachable in source, in progress means unfinished, planned means agreed direction, and needs design means a decision or playtest is required. These statuses do not certify current checks, visual polish or release availability. Supplied hero animation artwork remains TEST material pending visible playtesting.

## Now

**Opening readability — implemented; player acceptance deferred.** Combat, action feedback and traversal are implemented across the connected orchard → clearing → forge → chapel → orchard journey. Agent review covers rendered action/effect joins, interruption and queue feedback, route framing and reward/checkpoint persistence. Implementation and agent-driven journeys do not establish first-time player understanding.

Keep the [combat](game-design.md#combat-feel), [Burn](game-design.md#damage-types-and-burn) and [progression](game-design.md#character-progression) defaults until observed play supports a change. Preserve the blocked raised workshop and altar until elevation is authored. The opening does not need a chapter ending.

- **Enemy combat — implemented; readability acceptance pending.** Authored windups, contact effects, recovery, pursuit and linked encounters are in place. The warden retains physical poise; the lantern interrupts it, with the hurt pose spanning the existing stagger. Review these distinctions during grouped combat without ground attack zones. Follow [combat feel](game-design.md#combat-feel) and [opening guidance](game-design.md#opening-guidance).
- **Scene cohesion and traversal — implemented; player acceptance pending.** Scene-specific props, painted lighting, contact and cast shadows, selective glow, occlusion and matching return paths are in place. Review them together at gameplay scale. Keep the workshop and altar blocked; opening either route is a separate elevation-authoring decision. Follow [scene cohesion](scene-design.md#scene-cohesion) and [world structure](game-design.md#world-structure).
- **UI feedback and settings — implemented; usability acceptance pending.** Action animation, a temporary named queue cue and cooldown rejection distinguish admission, buffering and unavailable actions. Reduced motion keeps the queued aim marker steady. Save/load feedback, keyboard/pointer controls, inventory comparisons and compact layouts are implemented; review their clarity during ordinary play. Follow [action feedback](game-design.md#player-action-feedback), [equipment](game-design.md#equipment) and [menu contracts](architecture.md#change-player-menus-or-settings).

The agent pass ends after rendered combat/route review, focused regressions and the required source checks. The uncoached [first-time player playtest](verification.md#opening-playtest) is explicitly deferred until a fresh player is available; it remains open. Audible mix approval and target Windows acceptance remain separate pending checks. Use subsequent observations to reopen specific work rather than keeping an unbounded polish phase.

## Next

Address the largest observed combat or navigation friction before extending the adventure. Music and weaknesses remain deferred.

- **Next adventure milestone — needs design.** Choose a bounded destination, encounter, authored reward and stopping point, with a clear route into and out of the existing adventure. Define the scope before producing another scene; destinations and bosses remain uncommitted. Follow [world structure](game-design.md#world-structure).
- **Persistent world conditions — future direction.** Start with one authored shortcut, milestone or mechanism that must survive travel, reload and encounter respawn. Give GameSession a small typed checkpoint condition and connect it to the relevant interaction/navigation behavior. Reuse existing inventory or claim state when sufficient; no general quest framework. Follow [world structure](game-design.md#world-structure).
- **Progression and equipment expansion — needs design.** Build on [universal upgrades](game-design.md#universal-upgrades) with deliberately authored higher ranks and rewards. Define meaningful equipment effects, later upgrades and recipes, including whether Bone has a deliberate use. Keep currently cosmetic slots cosmetic until their effects and comparisons are designed; do not add recipes solely to consume an unused material. Follow [character progression](game-design.md#character-progression) and [equipment](game-design.md#equipment).

## Later

Extend the adventure and authored rewards, equipment, upgrades and encounters after combat polish and the first progression loop. Destinations, bosses and speculative systems remain uncommitted; see [world structure](game-design.md#world-structure).

- **Windows acceptance — deferred; reference hardware needs design.** Name reference CPU/GPU/display and validate [packaged 1440p/60 FPS](release.md#opening-windows-acceptance). macOS and software smoke do not certify target performance.
- **Performance and lifetime — deferred.** Warm up, measure roughly one minute in a seeded representative encounter and a modest stress fixture; record median/p95/stalls, environment, buffer size and scale. Account separately for downloads, decoded CPU data, GPU textures/mips, render targets and temporary allocations; verify settled growth across repeated room changes. Follow [performance comparisons](verification.md#performance-comparisons).
- **Release distribution — planned for later.** Complete Windows signing, installers, licenses/notices and Steam depot delivery. Validate an actual Steam beta installation, launch, update and overlay on supported hosts. Existing package and direct-executable checks do not establish distribution readiness. Follow [milestone delivery](release.md#milestone-delivery) and [Steam package validation](release.md#build-and-verify).

## Open design decisions

Lantern shape, cooldown, stagger, Burn cadence and recovery cancellation are chosen playtest defaults in the linked design sections. Weaknesses remain deferred; resolve their values and the progression relationships before extending those systems. Keep decisions in [Game design](game-design.md), then update the corresponding work here.
