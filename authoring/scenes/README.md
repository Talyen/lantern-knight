# Authored scenes

Save scene documents here through the [scene editor](../../docs/developer-tools.md#scene-editor). Scenes are replaceable prototype content; the selected orchard, clearing, forge and chapel documents supply Game content, while engine tests own independent fixtures.

Game starts in Abandoned Orchard and travels through Forest Gathering Clearing to Ruined Craftsman Forge and Desecrated Chapel. Open a draft in the editor and choose Play to run it without writing Game checkpoints. [Game content registration](../../docs/architecture.md#add-an-area-or-enemy) is a separate step. Sandbox rendering fixtures live in content code and are independent of this directory.

Saving a scene with ground interaction also retains an immutable revision under `authoring/ground-profiles/`. See [Saved ground profiles](../../docs/developer-tools.md#saved-ground-profiles) for restoration and artwork reassignment, and [scene design](../../docs/scene-design.md) for composition guidance and the starting painting scales.

## Orchard composition

The painted orchard remains the main environment. Its existing trees, fruit boxes, fallen timber, walls and cider press supply the landmarks; added props support four specific activities:

- **Camp verge:** a surviving fire and unused kindling sit behind bedding pulled toward the southern gate. A traveller’s satchel sits beside the bedding, catching the firelight: someone abandoned packing while the fire was still burning. The satchel holds a Traveller’s Charm (+5 maximum health when equipped in the trinket slot).
- **Harvest apron:** a parked wooden handcart, one cider barrel, tied sack and driver's roll sit below the painted harvest boxes. The eastern passage stays open and quiet; the driver’s roll is scenery rather than another reward cache.
- **Western picking station:** a wooden wheelbarrow, spare picking crate, sack and worker's bag relate to the existing empty fruit box and fallen timber. They leave the western approach available for fighting. One isolated skeleton guards salvaged iron here as an optional detour.
- **Press apron:** a packing crate, small supply sack and keeper's tool roll extend the shed's illustrated storage stack. The painting already supplies the press and larger equipment; added furniture would crowd its approach. Two skeletons guard the keeper’s tools and Orchard Ring; the packing crate supplies one recovery potion. No freestanding treasure chests or decorative scatter remain.

Each object's `purpose`, `label` and `zone` record its role. Keep those descriptions specific to its actual location. The main dirt routes stay open, supplies remain fully visible and enemies stand in clear pockets; neither painted cover nor a numerical encounter target justifies hiding gameplay. Check the complete painting and each work area at gameplay scale after changes. Original collision remains intact. The harvest-apron bush has a native-contour mask and depth boundary, so it covers the rear of the parked handcart and nearby driver’s roll.

The entrance is free of enemies. The nine-unit arrival frame reveals the camp while keeping the first skeleton outside the opening view, then narrows to normal gameplay distance farther inside. The first skeleton waits in the open pocket west of the apple canopy, with room to dodge and a short pursuit leash. It becomes fully visible on the approach from camp before its awareness activates. The main route then meets the press pair and linked departure pair; the picking station is optional. Guiding wind suggests the nearby abandoned satchel, the first enemy, the press supplies and their dropped reward, then departure. It yields during combat, allows the camp and first encounter to be bypassed, and does not rewind after resting once the press supplies are claimed. Most enemies carry no reward; the first drops one potion, departure yields gold and salvage, and optional supplies retain distinct uses.

## Clearing and forge composition

The clearing uses the central raised island to split the approach to the ritual stair apron. Two linked guards stand close enough to join the same fight, protecting the cache and the northwestern forge path. The cache rewards a Gatherer’s Seal (+10 maximum health in the trinket slot), replacing the optional orchard charm. The southern return path and camp remain available. A small watch fire sits west of the cache, drawing attention away from the blocked ritual stairs toward a second fire farther along the forge path. This pair uses the existing light budget and leaves the route’s walking width and matching return arrival open. The elevated altar and terraces stay blocked pending authored elevation.

Ruined Craftsman Forge begins at the southern entrance beside a surviving fire. The wall island divides the approach to the courtyard; a linked pair protects a salvaged tool chest on the visible courtyard side of the wall, with a separate watch near the northern building. The chest supplies Gold, Iron and one unique Workshop Temper. An intact workbench on clear courtyard ground offers Temper Sword or Rekindle Lantern, following [character progression](../../docs/game-design.md#character-progression). The bench sits on the entrance side of the cache with surviving smithing coals beside it, separating its usable work area from the blocked workshop. Iron stock rests on its tabletop, and a stump-mounted anvil beside the bench identifies the working station from the entrance. Both use existing prepared artwork and leave its approach open. The raised workshop remains blocked.

On-foot returns arrive inside the matching path: forge to the clearing’s western watch fire, then clearing to the orchard’s departure approach. These arrivals sit beyond immediate exit activation range. Campfire travel still goes directly to discovered fires. After an upgrade and collection of the forge cache rewards, guiding wind points toward the chapel. If the player instead backtracks, the clearing suggests the orchard return after its cache rewards are collected, and the orchard suggests camp after its press supplies are claimed. Collecting the Vigil Amulet takes priority over skipped caches and guides the full return, as described below. Rest before retracing encounters with the strengthened kit; claimed caches and the unique temper stay spent.

## Desecrated Chapel composition

The southwestern doorway and threshold fire lead into the existing nave painting. The supplied Sepulcher Warden, an armored skeleton carrying a two-handed hammer, holds the central aisle; two faster attendants converge from the western and eastern side aisles under shared awareness. Their leashes span the nave so a flank approach does not cancel the opposite attendant’s alarm. Its body brightens once during the latter half of the committed windup, alongside the existing small foot accent; neither marks an attack area. It resists physical interruption but remains vulnerable to the lantern’s stagger. Use the aisle junctions to gather attendants, then separate from the slower warden. Its overhead hammer hold lasts until the committed windup ends; the fourth supplied attack drawing begins at the damage tick in all four attack facings. Its longer swing gives room to dodge and counter rather than repeatedly interrupting it with sword hits.

A guarded reliquary on the altar approach rewards the Vigil Amulet, potions and Gold once. The altar platform, pews and column bases retain the existing collision and occlusion. The entrance fire supports retries; the southwestern retreat remains available throughout. The forge’s guarded northern street opens this route, and its matching return entry sits outside immediate exit range. Actor size, contact shadows and full-art placement clearance use the same visual scale. Existing library artwork supplies the encounter; original painting and character source files remain unchanged.

After collecting the Vigil Amulet, guiding wind follows the chapel, forge and clearing return exits, ending at Orchard Campfire. Skipped optional caches do not restart the outward route; loose rewards from an opened container remain a nearby stop.
