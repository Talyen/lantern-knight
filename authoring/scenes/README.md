# Authored scenes

Save scene documents here through the [scene editor](../../docs/developer-tools.md#scene-editor). Scenes are replaceable prototype content; the selected orchard, clearing, forge and chapel documents supply Game content, while engine tests own independent fixtures.

Game starts in Abandoned Orchard and travels through Forest Gathering Clearing to Ruined Craftsman Forge and Desecrated Chapel. Open a draft in the editor and choose Play to run it without writing Game checkpoints. [Game content registration](../../docs/architecture.md#add-an-area-or-enemy) is a separate step. Sandbox rendering fixtures live in content code and are independent of this directory.

Saving a scene with ground interaction also retains an immutable revision under `authoring/ground-profiles/`. See [Saved ground profiles](../../docs/developer-tools.md#saved-ground-profiles) for restoration and artwork reassignment, and [scene design](../../docs/scene-design.md) for composition guidance and the starting painting scales.

## Orchard composition

The painted orchard remains the main environment. Its existing trees, fruit boxes, fallen timber, walls and cider press supply the landmarks; added props support four specific activities:

- **Camp verge:** split orchard packing boards feed the low fire beside the southern wall. The new orchard backpack rests against the same wall within its light and holds the Traveller’s Charm (+5 maximum health when equipped).
- **Harvest apron:** one scene-specific cider barrel stands beside the painted fruit boxes. The cart, loose sacks and driver’s roll have been removed; the eastern passage stays open.
- **Western picking station:** a matching picker’s basket sits beside the painted fruit box station. The basket holds salvaged iron, guarded by the isolated western skeleton. The barrow and extra sacks have been removed.
- **Press apron:** a slatted breakable crate and the keeper’s packed crate sit beside the shed’s painted storage stack. The slatted crate supplies one recovery potion; the guarded crate holds the Orchard Ring and Gold. Both use the orchard set’s supplied animation and permanent spent drawing. The smaller packing crates flank the painted storage stack without covering its frontage.

Each object's `purpose`, `label` and `zone` record its location. The props use their scene-specific native artwork and supplied ground registration, with sizes reviewed beside the hero and the painted storage containers. Small picking baskets, portable toolboxes and travel packs retain their hand-carried proportions. Low fires use spent scene-specific wooden props beneath the shared flame effect instead of freestanding metal braziers. Main dirt routes and fighting pockets remain open. Original collision and painted occlusion remain intact. Check the complete painting and each work area at gameplay scale after changes.

The entrance is free of enemies. The nine-unit arrival frame reveals the camp while keeping the first skeleton outside the opening view, then narrows to normal gameplay distance farther inside. The first skeleton waits in the open pocket west of the apple canopy, with room to dodge and a short pursuit leash. It becomes fully visible on the approach from camp before its awareness activates. The main route then meets the press pair and linked departure pair; the picking station is optional. Guiding wind suggests the nearby abandoned backpack, the first enemy, the press supplies and their dropped reward, then departure. It yields during combat, allows the camp and first encounter to be bypassed, and does not rewind after resting once the press supplies are claimed. Most enemies carry no reward; the first drops one potion, departure yields gold and salvage, and optional supplies retain distinct uses.

## Clearing and forge composition

The clearing uses the central raised island to split the approach to the ritual stair apron. Two linked guards stand close enough to join the same fight, protecting the cache and the northwestern forge path. The cache rewards a Gatherer’s Seal (+10 maximum health in the trinket slot), replacing the optional orchard charm. The southern return path and camp remain available. The scene-specific ochre chest rests below the stair-side rock ledge, clear of the central fighting pocket. A matching provision hamper sits beside the southern camp’s rock verge. The two isolated watch fires have been removed. The walking route and matching return arrival remain open. The elevated altar and terraces stay blocked pending authored elevation.

Ruined Craftsman Forge begins at the southern entrance beside a surviving fire. The wall island divides the approach to the courtyard; a linked pair protects a salvaged tool chest on the visible courtyard side of the wall, with a separate watch near the northern building. The chest supplies Gold and Iron. Kit improvements are available at every campfire, following [character progression](../../docs/game-design.md#character-progression). The portable wooden toolbox retains the salvage rewards beside the collapsed workshop entrance and its stone rubble. The bellows prop has been removed from the scene and source library. The former freestanding workbench, anvil, iron stack and marker braziers have been removed. Spent charcoal-crate timber feeds the southern resting fire. The raised workshop remains blocked.

On-foot returns arrive inside the matching path: forge to the clearing’s western path, then clearing to the orchard’s departure approach. These arrivals sit beyond immediate exit activation range. Campfire travel still goes directly to discovered fires. After collection of the forge cache rewards, guiding wind points toward the chapel. If the player instead backtracks, the clearing suggests the orchard return after its cache rewards are collected, and the orchard suggests camp after its press supplies are claimed. Collecting the Vigil Amulet takes priority over skipped caches and guides the full return, as described below. Rest before retracing encounters with the strengthened kit; claimed caches stay spent and purchased improvements remain owned.

## Desecrated Chapel composition

The southwestern doorway and threshold fire lead into the existing nave painting. The supplied Sepulcher Warden, an armored skeleton carrying a two-handed hammer, holds the central aisle; two faster attendants converge from the western and eastern side aisles under shared awareness. Their leashes span the nave so a flank approach does not cancel the opposite attendant’s alarm. Its body brightens once during the latter half of the committed windup, alongside the existing small foot accent; neither marks an attack area. It resists physical interruption but remains vulnerable to the lantern’s stagger. Use the aisle junctions to gather attendants, then separate from the slower warden. Its overhead hammer hold lasts until the committed windup ends; the fourth supplied attack drawing begins at the damage tick in all four attack facings. Its longer swing gives room to dodge and counter rather than repeatedly interrupting it with sword hits.

A guarded scene-specific stone altar coffer on the altar approach rewards the Vigil Amulet, potions and Gold once. It uses the supplied opening sequence and permanently opened drawing. Splintered chapel furniture feeds the low threshold fire; the freestanding marker brazier has been removed. The altar platform, pews and column bases retain the existing collision and occlusion. The entrance fire supports retries; the southwestern retreat remains available throughout. The forge’s guarded northern street opens this route, and its matching return entry sits outside immediate exit range. Actor size, contact shadows and full-art placement clearance use the same visual scale. Existing library artwork supplies the encounter; original painting and character source files remain unchanged.

After collecting the Vigil Amulet, guiding wind follows the chapel, forge and clearing return exits, ending at Orchard Campfire. Skipped optional caches do not restart the outward route; loose rewards from an opened container remain a nearby stop.

## Woodland composition

The travel pack rests at the stone footing of the northern ruined gatepost, off the trail. Its hand-carried size is reviewed beside the hero. Woodland uses Silver hour because its source painting already contains deep cool shade; this preserves readable ground beyond the stronger warm lantern pool. The other populated scenes retain Lantern night. Game and the editor consume the same saved look.
