# Placement lessons

No human preferences have been recorded for this loop yet. The skill's initial composition guidance comes from the existing scene policy and remains a starting point for evaluation.

Keep only compact, useful lessons here. Raw sessions, screenshots, rejected variants and detailed history stay outside Git. For each retained lesson record:

- The observable placement decision and the scene conditions where it helps.
- Status: hypothesis, supported, contradicted, or retired.
- Practice and holdout round IDs, winning variant, round hashes, and the external session location. Include losses and contrary results.
- What was held fixed, what changed, and the limits of the evidence.

A single preference supports that image, not an explanation of the human's reasoning. Require another controlled comparison in a different scene before promoting a general rule into the skill. Keep useful scene-specific decisions scoped to that scene. Retire instructions that repeatedly lose instead of accumulating exceptions.
